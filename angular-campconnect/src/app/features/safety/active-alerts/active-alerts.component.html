Winter Storm Warning - Grand Teton

Critical
Grand Teton NP, WY Valid until February 5, 2026

Major winter storm expected to bring 18-24 inches of snow and winds up to 60 mph. Whiteout conditions likely. Travel not recommended.

Affected Areas

  • All roads and trails
  • Backcountry areas
  • Campgrounds

Recommendations

  • Postpone all travel plans
  • Shelter in place if already in area
  • Carry emergency supplies if travel is unavoidable
  • Monitor weather forecasts continuously
Issued: February 2, 2026

Stage 2 Fire Restrictions - Rocky Mountain National Park

High
Rocky Mountain NP, CO Valid until March 15, 2026

Elevated fire danger due to dry conditions and high winds. Stage 2 restrictions are in effect for all areas of Rocky Mountain National Park and surrounding National Forest lands.

Affected Areas

  • All backcountry camping areas
  • Designated campgrounds
  • Day use areas

Recommendations

  • Use only camp stoves with shut-off valves
  • Never leave stoves unattended
  • Report any smoke or fire immediately to 911
Issued: February 1, 2026

Trail Closure - Avalanche Danger

High
Rocky Mountain NP - Longs Peak Trail Valid until April 1, 2026

Longs Peak Trail closed due to extreme avalanche danger. Several avalanche paths cross the trail above treeline. Violators will be cited.

Affected Areas

  • Longs Peak Trail above treeline
  • Boulder Field area

Recommendations

  • Choose alternative lower elevation trails
  • Never enter closed areas
  • Wait for official all-clear announcement
Issued: January 15, 2026

Increased Bear Activity - Yosemite Valley

Medium
Yosemite Valley, CA Valid until April 30, 2026

Multiple bear sightings reported in campground areas. Bears are actively seeking food as they emerge from winter dens. Proper food storage is mandatory.

Affected Areas

  • Upper Pines Campground
  • Valley trails

Recommendations

  • Use bear-proof food lockers at all times
  • Carry bear spray on trails
  • Report all bear sightings to rangers
Issued: January 28, 2026

Water Quality Advisory - Lake Camping

Low
Multiple Colorado Wilderness Areas Valid until Ongoing

Giardia detected in several alpine lakes. Always treat or filter water before drinking.

Affected Areas

  • All backcountry water sources
  • Alpine lakes

Recommendations

  • Boil water for 1 minute minimum
  • Use certified water filter (0.2 micron or smaller)
  • Never drink untreated water
Issued: January 20, 2026
